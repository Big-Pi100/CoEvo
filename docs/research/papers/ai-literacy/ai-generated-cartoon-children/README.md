# AI-Generated Cartoon on YouTube Kids: Pacing, Sensory Intensity and Toddler Attention

**Source:** Iconic Research And Engineering Journals (IRE Journals), Volume 9 Issue 11, May 2026
**Authors:** Pritpal Kaur, Dr. Bhargavi D. Hemmige
**Link:** https://www.irejournals.com/paper-details/1718221

## Key Findings

- AI-generated cartoons on YouTube Kids are characterized by: fast pacing, highly-saturated colors, quick fast cuts, continuation errors, and distortion — all designed to sustain attention
- Parents report toddlers becoming "glued to the screen" and more aggressive/unsettled when AI cartoons are turned off
- Limited study has been done on how AI-generated cartoons negatively affect toddler cognitive development and disrupt/shorten attention span
- Qualitative analysis of 15 AI-generated cartoon clips from YouTube Kids

## Implications for CoEvo

- Direct evidence that AI-generated content designed for maximum engagement (not learning) negatively impacts children's cognitive development
- Supports the thesis that children need objective verification systems to distinguish quality content from "junk content"
- Connects to the "清朗·未成年人网络保护" policy narrative — AI content moderation alone is insufficient; children need metacognitive skills
